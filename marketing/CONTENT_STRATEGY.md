# Organic Content Strategy

## Current starting point — 16 August 2026

- 1,393 live products across 15 categories
- Strongest immediate content advantage: store comparison, price freshness, recorded history, saved products, trending and deals discovery
- Multi-store coverage remains limited; single-store products must not be presented as broad market comparisons
- Zero-budget organic distribution only

## Initial mix

- 30% verified comparisons/deals
- 25% shopping education and fake-discount checks
- 20% price-history explainers
- 15% category/budget guides
- 10% PriceVichar feature education

## Initial experiments

1. Trust-first hook versus savings-first hook
2. 12–16 second videos versus 20–25 second videos
3. CTA “PriceVichar par compare karo” versus “Buy se pehle price check karo”

## Publishing gate

Do not publish a deal when the retailer page does not independently expose the claimed current price or availability. Use an educational post instead.
