# PriceVichar Social Brand Guide

## Positioning

PriceVichar is an independent, customer-first Indian product and price-discovery platform. It helps shoppers compare available store prices, understand recorded price information, and confirm the final offer on the retailer site.

**Primary slogan:** “Search. Compare. Think. Buy better.”

**Supporting line:** “Everything you need to make a smarter choice—right here on PriceVichar.”

**Primary CTA:** “PriceVichar par latest price check karo.”

**Website:** `https://pricevichar.com`

## Voice

- Practical, calm Hinglish
- Useful before promotional
- No fake urgency or unsupported superlatives
- Say “current listed price” or “recorded low” only when the evidence supports it
- Always remind viewers that prices and availability can change

## Visual system

- Format: 1080×1920 vertical
- Primary: deep navy `#07183F`
- Accent: royal blue `#1769E8`
- Trust accent: emerald green `#14B86A`
- Supporting: white `#FFFFFF`, soft blue-gray `#F4F7FF`
- Keep critical text inside a 120 px side margin and away from platform UI zones

### Style direction — 17 August 2026

Decided with Mithun: new videos use an **illustration hybrid** style, not flat UI-card-only frames (the earlier Mivi DuoPods Oris video is flat UI-card and stays as the baseline to compare against).

- Background/hook elements: soft illustrated shapes, simple flat-illustration characters or product staging (podium, floating elements) — warm, blush/lavender-leaning palette, not corporate-flat
- Price/data must never be illustrated or hand-drawn — real numbers (price, store name, rating, "X din check") always render as a clean typographic card/pill on top of the illustration, same legibility bar as the current UI-card videos
- Reasoning: illustration reads as warmer and more scroll-stopping for the hook, while the data card keeps the actual price/comparison trustworthy and legible — never sacrifice number legibility for style
- Reference example: `marketing/assets/2026-08-17-realme-buds-illustration.mp4` (Realme Buds 3 comparison, first illustration-hybrid video), built from `marketing/create_realme_illustration_frames.py` + `marketing/render_motion_slideshow.swift`

## Required disclosure

Use a readable variant of: “Prices/availability can change. Final price retailer par check karein.” If an affiliate link is used, clearly disclose it.

## Marketing rule

Never claim that every displayed price changed that day. The site checks its catalog daily, but retailer availability or a temporary store failure can leave the last verified price unchanged. Use “prices checked daily,” not “all prices updated daily.”
